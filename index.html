About

I'm a frontend developer based in San Francisco with 5+ years of experience building products used by millions. I specialize in React ecosystems, performance engineering, and design systems.

Before going independent, I was a Senior Engineer at Stripe, where I helped rebuild the Dashboard's component library. I believe great UI is invisible — it gets out of the way so people can do what they came to do.

When I'm not pushing pixels, I'm writing about web performance on my blog, contributing to open source, and exploring generative art with canvas and WebGL.
